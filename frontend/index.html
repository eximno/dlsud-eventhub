DLSUD EventHub — a 4th-year BSIT laboratory prototype. Not an official De La Salle University-Dasmarinas system, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the university. All events and students shown are sample data.

The database runs entirely in your browser through SQLite compiled to WebAssembly, so nothing you type is sent to a server — and nothing stored here is secure or authoritative. Project README and documentation
